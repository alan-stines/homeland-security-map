Understand the coverage

These feeds do not cover every emergency, crime, security incident, outage, or evacuation order. No national threat level is inferred. Follow official source links and local authorities for protective actions. NWS polygons may use forecast-zone boundaries; unresolved areas stay in the list rather than receiving an invented location. Earthquake magnitudes are not impact ratings. Wildfire size and containment do not establish public safety. River gauge categories describe conditions at a gauge, not the extent of flooding.
